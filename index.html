About Me

I'm a Senior Product Manager and 2x startup co-founder with a passion for turning ideas into scalable products. I thrive in fast-paced environments where relationships, execution, and bold thinking matter.

Over the past few years, I've launched a fintech venture from scratch to a SAR 100M valuation in just 90 days, secured SAR 20M+ in public sector deals, and built multiple platforms that now serve clients. I've also been recognized globally at the Entrepreneurship World Cup (Top 100 out of 16,000 startups).

My strengths lie in energizing teams, building strong stakeholder relationships, and pushing ideas into reality quickly. I lead with intuition, creativity, and action—and I work best when paired with organized, detail-driven teams that bring structure to vision.
